IM Friend Baptist Church's vision

50

Evangelizing All 50 States

Our mission does not stop in Texas. Just as Matthew 24:14 declares, we proclaim the gospel until all people hear and become disciples of Jesus Christ. For this reason, our first goal is to see the gospel spread throughout all 50 states of the United States.

Planting 500 Churches

A church that teaches the Word and raises disciples. We pray that such churches will rise up through IM Friend Baptist Church. With a vision to establish 500 churches across the nation, we seek to build communities where faith is passed on to the next generation.

Training 5,000 Missionaries

As disciples of Jesus, we are missionaries wherever we go! We strive to leave the mark of Christ in every place He sends us. It is our goal at IM Friend Baptist Church to raise and send out 5,000 missionaries who will carry the gospel to the world.
